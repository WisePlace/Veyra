Click the Veyra Connect icon in your toolbar, then Send session to Veyra.

Easiest: in the Veyra Connect extension popup, click Copy session cookie, then paste it below.

Or grab it manually from your browser:

  1. In the browser where you're logged into your burner account, open photos.google.com.
  2. Open the developer tools: press F12 (or right-click then Inspect).
  3. Select the Network tab, then reload the page.
  4. In the list of requests, click the first row whose name is photos.google.com.
  5. Open the Headers sub-tab and scroll to Request Headers.
  6. Find the row starting with cookie:, right-click its value then Copy value.
  7. Paste it in the box below and click Connect.
